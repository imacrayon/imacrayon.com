---
title: Shine
date: 2023-10-15
media: acrylic on canvas
dimentions: 40" × 30"
src: /img/pictures/shine/shine.jpg
alt: An abstract painting containing a large centered rectangle with a deep golden ombré color. The rectangle is almost entirely obscured in a cloud of cool-colored, large gestural strokes except for its bottom right corner. The top right quadrant of the canvas contains dark purple stokes emanating from the rectangle with a smoke-like quality. The top left quadrant contains a puddle of harsh pinks and purples that swirl and drip down the full length of the canvas into an angular dark cloud of purple in the bottom left quadrant.
owner: Carmen Aratea
